Weekly salad meal menu

Our pure vegetarian menu rotates over 5 weeks, Monday to Saturday. Two menus run each day, so you eat a different bowl through the month.

Week 1 is the Monday to Saturday week that contains the 1st of the month. The menu can change with seasonal produce, and each day's menu is also sent on WhatsApp. Jain and Swaminarayan versions are made by changing ingredients. Vegan meals follow their own menu, see the Vegan plan.
