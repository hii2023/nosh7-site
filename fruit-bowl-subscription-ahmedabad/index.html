Questions

What is in a NOSH7 fruit bowl?

Each bowl is 400 grams of 7 to 8 fresh-cut seasonal and exotic fruits, chosen from apple, banana, kiwi, dragon fruit, pomegranate, mosambi, papaya, chikoo and pineapple. Nothing is added: no sugar, no syrup, no cream.

How much does the fruit bowl subscription cost?

A week of 5 bowls is Rs 1,250 (Rs 250 a bowl). A month of 25 bowls is Rs 5,250 (Rs 210 a bowl). Delivery is free within 5 km of Iscon Cross Road, then Rs 10 per km.

How many calories are in a fruit bowl?

About 220 calories. It is rich in vitamins, minerals and fibre and has no added sugar, so it works as a light breakfast or an evening snack.

When is the fruit bowl delivered?

Pick one of three daily batches when you order: Breakfast 9:30 to 11:30 AM, Lunch 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM or Dinner 6 to 8 PM. You can skip, pause or change the slot in the NOSH7 app up to 2 hours before delivery.

Is the fruit bowl available in a Jain version?

Jain is not offered on the Fruit Dish Pack. If you are on a meal plan, you can also add a fruit bowl to any meal for Rs 169.

Is a fruit bowl suitable for diabetics?

Fruit contains natural sugar. If you are managing diabetes, the Low Sugar meal plan is made for that, and please check with your doctor about fruit in your diet.
