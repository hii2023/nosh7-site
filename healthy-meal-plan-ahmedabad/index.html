Questions

What is the Healthy Fresh plan?

Our everyday balanced meal plan: a different pure veg salad bowl each day, about 425 calories with 20g or more protein, high fibre and low carbs, delivered in the batch you pick.

Is Healthy Fresh good for weight loss?

It is a complete low calorie meal, so it fits a weight loss diet. If weight loss is your main goal, the Weight Loss plan adds more protein and 50g paneer to every bowl.

Is this a subscription?

Yes. You pay for a pack of meals up front (5 for the trial, 25 for a month) and we deliver one meal on each day you pick, Monday to Friday by default with Saturday optional, until the pack is used. Nothing renews by itself: you recharge in the app when you want more. Every meal must be used within 3 months.

Can I pause, skip or change my delivery?

Yes, in the NOSH7 app, at least 2 hours before your batch starts. Pause and resume, skip a day, change your batch time or delivery address. A skipped or paused delivery stays in your balance.

What are the delivery timings?

Breakfast 9:30 to 11:30 AM, Lunch 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM or Dinner 5 to 8 PM. You pick one when you order and can change it later in the app.

Where do you deliver?

From our kitchen in Satellite, free within 5 km of Iscon Cross Road (Satellite, Prahlad Nagar, Bodakdev, Vastrapur) and Rs 10 per km beyond, to Thaltej, Bopal, Navrangpura, Paldi and more. Enter your address at checkout to see the exact fee.
